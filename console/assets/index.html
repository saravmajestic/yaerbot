Land

Plain land seeds on a fixed grid from the plan below. Drip land follows the lateral and plants at each emitter the camera finds — the spacing there is only an estimate for counting.

Mark the plot

Walk the boundary and mark each corner in order. 1 = where the robot starts · 2 = far end of the first row (it drives 1→2 straight, then snakes across to 3 and 4).

Spacing

Emitters

Seeds are placed where the camera sees an emitter. This figure only estimates the count and stops one emitter being planted twice — it doesn't need to be exact.

With more than one, the robot searches for the next lateral at the end of a row: turn, drive across until the camera sees a crossing tube, turn onto it. It needs no row spacing and the laterals need not be parallel. It gives up after about three row-gaps of searching.

Seed placement

Pick the arm positions to plant at. Each one drops 2 seeds (the outlets sit 180° apart), so 0° + 90° makes a 4-seed cross. The robot stops once per emitter and works through them in order.

Camera

USB camera: enter usb — it finds the camera by capability, because the /dev/video index moves between the webcam and the board's hardware codec across replugs. WiFi camera: farmcam.local. Frames are always 320×240 — every detector threshold is calibrated at that size, and the USB camera streams it natively.

Connect to the camera (type usb, or farmcam.local) to see the annotated feed.
